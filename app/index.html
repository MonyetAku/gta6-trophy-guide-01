    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, one playthrough. GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so the saves you keep are the safety net — and one trophy has a step that can be missed. Sources: the official trophy list (PlayStation, as listed by Exophase / PSNProfiles) and Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List, checked 19 Nov 2026.

    1. Stage 1 · Chapter 1 — set your saves

      Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave. Do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (after she is home, check the kitchen in the morning — a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time — and follow her to the balcony of Jason's house): it counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and is gone once Chapter 1 ends. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they should switch off both trophies and saving.

    2. Stage 2 · Chapter 2 — partners and apps

      Switching between Jason and Lucia opens up, and from here you can call the other in as your partner — several trophies need the two together. The Vice City Real Estate app (buy all 8 garages, about $160,000) and the Scooter Bros app (register 10 vehicles) unlock here, as does the Kingpin Bowling illegal operation. Sample every activity type once for Master of None.

    3. Stage 3 · Chapters 3–4 — robberies and races

      Chapter 3 hands you the Snap Gun (Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs) for jewelry robberies and holds the time-sensitive street race Race Against Yago — save first, race the moment it appears. Near the end of Chapter 4, the optional mission That's Where The Money Is opens bank vaults: bring your partner, and escaping a vault with them earns Hot Together too. Story mission trophies pop as you play; chapter trophies can pop at the start of the next chapter.

    4. Stage 4 · Final mission and side finales

      Finish the story, then the five side-story finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) — they stay open after the credits, and Andy's, Billy's and Tyrone's strands do not count toward Strangers & Freaks. Bret's hunting questline (Jason-only) and the 8 stranger strands finish here too.

    5. Stage 5 · After the credits — 100% and the grinds

      The 100% checklist (35 tasks) appears under Progress, Stats, 100% Completion — work you did earlier still counts. Clean up the long grinds: 300 stunts, 70 of 80 Challenges, 200 of 277 vehicle models, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 illegal operations, 42 shooting-range gold medals, 100 WAiNK locations, 3 card sets, 500 Criminal Profile actions. The platinum pops with the last one.
